# Keyur Marolia

### Equity Research & Financial Modelling

I focus on equity research, company analysis and valuation. My projects connect financial statements, business drivers and capital allocation to earnings forecasts, cash flows and market-implied expectations.

I use Excel, Python and SQL for financial modelling and investment research, with supporting work in credit risk and market risk.

## Selected projects

| Project | Business question | Evidence to explore |
|---|---|---|
| [Pricol Equity Research](https://github.com/keyurmarolia/pricol-equity-research) | What growth, reinvestment and profitability assumptions support equity value? | Three-case Excel forecast, DCF, reverse DCF and forward P/E comparison |
| [Cholamandalam Finance](https://github.com/keyurmarolia/cholamandalam-finance-equity-research) | What growth, credit costs and equity reinvestment support a lender's valuation? | Excel lender model, FCFE valuation, reverse-implied ROE and reconciliation review |
| [InterGlobe Aviation](https://github.com/keyurmarolia/interglobe-aviation-equity-research-model) | How do airline operating drivers, fleet investment and leases affect valuation? | Excel operating forecast, DCF, sensitivities and reverse DCF |
| [Momentum in Indian Equities](https://github.com/keyurmarolia/momentum-in-indian-equities-research) | How sensitive are momentum results to portfolio construction, costs and tax assumptions? | 180 configurations, saved comparisons and a local research dashboard |
| [IFRS 9 Mortgage ECL](https://github.com/keyurmarolia/ifrs9-mortgage-ecl) | How do credit deterioration, recoveries and scenarios affect expected losses? | PD/LGD/EAD modelling, staging, worked loan traces and an Excel report |
| [Basel Credit Capital](https://github.com/keyurmarolia/basel-credit-capital-engine) | How do SA and IRB translate a loan portfolio into RWA and capital requirements? | 30,000 synthetic exposures, loan-level calculations and stress analysis |
| [FRTB Market Risk](https://github.com/keyurmarolia/frtb-market-risk-engine) | How do trading-book risks and desk eligibility feed market-risk capital? | Shared synthetic book, SA/IMA calculations and fallback treatment |

Each project opens with its scope, results and exploration links. Saved notebooks and reports can be reviewed before installing dependencies. Data sources, synthetic assumptions and material limitations are stated within each repository.

## Technical focus

Excel for integrated financial statements, scenario analysis and valuation; Python for investment research and validation; SQL for data analysis and traceability.
